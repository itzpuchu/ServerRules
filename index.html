Server Rules

Disclaimers

The use of any third-party software that provides an unfair advantage is strictly prohibited and may result in punishment. Punishments vary depending on the severity of the offense and the player’s punishment history. The Founder reserves the right to issue a permanent removal from the network if it is determined that the player shows no intent to change their behavior.

Knowledge of Rules

Claiming ignorance of the rules is not a valid excuse. All rules listed are considered basic expectations commonly enforced across online games and applications used in everyday life. By playing on NovaQuest, you agree to follow these standards.

Rule Changes

Rules may be adjusted over time with input from the Management Team but will always be finalized by the Founder. Every effort is made to keep all rules clear, fair, and airtight.

Loopholes & Exploits

Attempting to exploit loopholes to bypass the rules is not permitted. Depending on the situation, this may result in punishment or the rule being revised to prevent further abuse.
