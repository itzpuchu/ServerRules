NovaQuest Rules & Guidelines

This guide is publicly available to show transparency, outline expectations for all players, and ensure the NovaQuest community remains safe, fair, and enjoyable. Staff and players alike are expected to follow these rules. Ignorance of rules will not be accepted as an excuse.

General Conduct

  • Respect all players, staff, and members of the community. Harassment, bullying, or hate speech will not be tolerated.
  • No spamming, advertising, or promoting other servers or services.
  • Keep all interactions professional and follow community standards both in-game and on official communication platforms.
  • Follow all staff instructions. Disobeying staff may result in strikes, temporary bans, or removal from the network.

Cheating & Unfair Advantage

Any software, modification, or tool that gives a player an advantage outside normal gameplay is strictly prohibited. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Hacks, cheats, or exploits (auto-clickers, macros, combat automation, X-ray, wall hacks, NUKER, etc.)
  • Movement modifications (flight, speed, or similar) that are not explicitly allowed for your rank or server permissions
  • Any other third-party software or tool that provides an unfair advantage, even if it is not listed here

Ignorance of this rule will not be accepted. Players are responsible for ensuring any tool or ability they use is explicitly allowed.

Alternative Accounts (Alts)

Using multiple accounts is only allowed with explicit permission from the Founder. Players sharing the same IP must create a support ticket to link accounts. Attempting to bypass restrictions without permission will result in punishment or network removal.

Server & User Attacks

Any action intended to harm, disrupt, or compromise the server or its members is strictly prohibited. This includes:

  • Lagging, crashing, or exploiting server stability
  • Malicious links, IP grabbers, or malware
  • DDoS attacks or Doxxing any member, staff, or server

Consequences:

  • Immediate IP ban from the network
  • Report to Mojang and/or server host if applicable
  • Permanent removal from NovaQuest for severe cases

Any threat of such attacks is treated with the same severity as an actual attack.

IRL Trading

Trading any in-game items, ranks, or currency for real-world money or goods is strictly prohibited. Attempting, suggesting, or offering such trades will result in **immediate network removal**, regardless of whether a deal is completed.

Gambling

Player-run casinos or gambling systems are strictly forbidden. Mentioning or promoting gambling anywhere on the server is prohibited, unless explicitly approved by NovaQuest (following EULA compliance). This includes chat, builds, mini-games, or creative implementations.

Players caught gaining an unfair advantage will be punished based on whether they participate in competitive modes:

  • Regular Players (non-competitive):
    • First offense: 3-day ban
    • Second offense: 7-day ban
    • Third offense: 14-day ban

    Stats will not be removed for regular players.

  • Competitive Players:
    • First offense: 3-day ban + stats removal + season competitive ban
    • Second offense: 7-day ban + stats removal + season competitive ban
    • Third offense: 14-day ban + stats removal + season competitive ban

These measures protect fairness and integrity for all players. Repeat violations may result in longer bans or permanent disqualification from competitive play.

  • Staff are held to higher standards than regular players.
  • Failure to comply with Staff Rules may result in immediate demotion or removal, depending on severity.
  • All staff actions must remain professional. Personal conflicts with players or other staff must be escalated to management.
  • Leaking information from tickets, staff chat, or management channels is strictly prohibited and may result in demotion or removal.
  • Staff may work for multiple games, but helpers must complete a 14-day trainee period before doing so.
  • Staff who find bugs must report them within 24 hours. Failure to report, especially if used for advantage, will result in a strike.
  • All promotions are decided by management and the Founder. Staff may not hint at or promise promotions.
  • Staff must prioritize duties over gameplay when on duty.
