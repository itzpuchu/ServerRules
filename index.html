NovaQuest

Official Server Rules

Disclaimer

Failure to read these rules is not an excuse. All players are expected to follow them at all times.

Rules may change to close loopholes, prevent abuse, or improve fairness.

General Behavior

  • Harassment, threats, hate speech, or discrimination are prohibited.
  • Excessive toxicity, baiting, or provoking players or staff is punishable.
  • Impersonating staff or other players is not allowed.
  • Encouraging rule-breaking counts as breaking rules yourself.

Unfair Advantage

Any tool, modification, method, or behavior that provides an unfair advantage over normal gameplay is prohibited.

  • Hacked or modified clients
  • Cheats (fly, speed, reach, x-ray, nuker, scaffold, etc.)
  • Macros or automation that removes player effort
  • Abusing bugs, glitches, or unintended mechanics
  • Using features outside your rank permissions

If something provides an advantage other players do not have, assume it is not allowed.

Competitive & Leaderboard Rules

Competitive players are held to stricter standards.

  • First offense: 3-day ban + stat wipe + competitive season ban
  • Second offense: 7-day ban + stat wipe + competitive season ban
  • Third offense: 14-day ban + stat wipe + competitive season ban

Non-competitive players receive bans without stat wipes, increasing per offense.

Alternate Accounts

  • Alternate accounts are not allowed without approval.
  • Shared IP households must open a ticket.
  • Using alts to bypass punishment results in escalated penalties.

Server & Player Attacks

  • Attempting to lag, crash, or exploit server stability
  • DDoS threats or attempts
  • Malicious links, IP grabbers, or doxxing

Violations result in IP bans, permanent removal, and reports to Mojang and hosting providers.

IRL Trading

Trading anything involving real-world money or external services is prohibited.

Attempting or suggesting IRL trading results in permanent network removal.

Gambling

  • Player-run casinos or betting systems are not allowed.
  • Advertising gambling is prohibited.
  • Only official NovaQuest EULA-compliant systems are permitted.

Final Notes

NovaQuest enforces these rules to maintain fairness, safety, and transparency. If unsure, ask staff before acting.
