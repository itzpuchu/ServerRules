NovaQuest

This server rules page is publicly available to show transparency and outline expectations for all players.

Disclaimers

Using any third-party software to gain an unfair advantage is strictly prohibited and may result in punishment. Repeat offenses may result in harsher penalties. The Founder reserves the right to permanently remove a player from the network if they show no intent to correct their behavior.

Claiming ignorance of the rules is not a valid excuse. All rules listed are basic expectations commonly enforced across games and online communities. By playing on NovaQuest, you agree to follow these standards.

Rules may be updated over time with input from the Management Team but will always be finalized by the Founder. Efforts are made to keep rules clear, fair, and airtight. Attempting to exploit loopholes or bypass rules is not allowed and may result in punishment or rule revisions.

Knowledge of Rules

All players are expected to understand and follow these rules. If you are unsure about a rule, it is your responsibility to ask before acting. Ignorance will not excuse violations.

Rule Changes

Rules may change over time with input from the Management Team but will always be finalized by the Founder. Updates will be communicated to the community to ensure transparency.

Loopholes & Exploits

Attempting to exploit loopholes or creative methods to bypass rules is prohibited. Such actions may result in punishment or rule revision to prevent further abuse.

Cheating & Unfair Advantage

Any software, modification, or tool that gives a player an advantage outside normal gameplay is strictly prohibited. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Hacks, cheats, or exploits (auto-clickers, macros, combat automation, X-ray, wall hacks, NUKER, etc.)
  • Movement modifications (flight, speed, or similar) that are not explicitly allowed for your rank or server permissions
  • Any other third-party software or tool that provides an unfair advantage, even if it is not listed here

Ignorance of this rule will not be accepted. Players are responsible for ensuring any tool or ability they use is explicitly allowed.

Alternative Accounts (Alts)

Use of multiple accounts is only allowed with explicit permission from the Founder. If multiple players share the same IP, you must create a support ticket so management can link your accounts. Attempting to bypass restrictions without permission will result in punishment or removal from the network.

Server & User Attacks

Any action intended to harm, disrupt, or compromise the server or its members is strictly prohibited. This includes:

  • Lagging, crashing, or attempting to exploit server stability
  • Malicious links, IP grabbers, or malware
  • DDoS attacks or Doxxing any member, staff, or server

Consequences:

  • Immediate IP ban from the network
  • Report to Mojang and/or server host if applicable
  • Permanent removal from NovaQuest for severe cases

Any threat of such attacks will be treated with the same severity as an actual attack.

IRL Trading

Trading any in-game items, ranks, or currency for real-world money or goods is strictly prohibited. Attempting, suggesting, or offering such trades will result in **immediate network removal**, regardless of whether a deal actually occurs.

Gambling

Player-run casinos or gambling systems are strictly forbidden. Mentioning or promoting gambling anywhere on the server is also prohibited, unless explicitly approved by NovaQuest (following EULA compliance). This includes text chat, builds, mini-games, or other creative implementations.
