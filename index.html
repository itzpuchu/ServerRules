NovaQuest

Official Server Rules

General Rule Policy

  • Claiming you did not read or know the rules is never an excuse.
  • Claiming something was “just a joke” is never an excuse.
  • If you are unsure whether something is allowed, you must ask staff first.
  • Rules may be updated at any time to close loopholes or prevent abuse.
  • Staff may punish harmful behavior even if it is not explicitly listed.

Island & Team Information

  • Transferring island ownership permanently gives full control to the new leader.
  • Team disputes are handled by island leaders unless rules are broken.
  • Team payouts are paid to the island leader to distribute.
  • Shared IP households must create a ticket before any punishment occurs.
  • Winner Titles require 14 days of participation before the final week.

Competitive & Leaderboards

  • Cheating, boosting, merging, or abuse may result in disqualification.
  • Entire islands may be penalized for one member’s violations.
  • Star penalties reduce stars by at least 25%.
  • Leaving a disqualified island requires a ticket and forfeiting non-store items.

Chat & Communication

  • No spam, excessive caps, symbols, or repeated messages.
  • No harassment, insults, toxicity, or baiting.
  • No hate speech, slurs, or discriminatory language.
  • No impersonating staff, creators, or public figures.
  • No fake server information or misleading messages.
  • No NSFW, sexual, political, or sensitive content.

Punishments

  • Warnings are used for minor offenses.
  • Mutes block global and linked Discord chat.
  • Mute evasion increases punishment.
  • Ban evasion results in all linked accounts being banned.
  • Repeated violations may result in community removal or blacklist.

Unfair Advantage

Any method that gives an unfair advantage over normal gameplay is prohibited.

  • Hacked or modified clients
  • Macros, automation, or AFK grinding
  • X-ray, nuker, fly, speed, combat mods
  • Bug abusing or exploiting unintended mechanics
  • Using features outside your rank permissions

If a modification is not listed, it does not mean it is allowed.

Economy Rules

  • No boosting players outside your team.
  • No large gifts intended to affect leaderboards.
  • No scamming, misleading trades, or false advertising.
  • No money loans or item renting.

Alternate Accounts

  • Account sharing is prohibited.
  • Alt limits vary by game mode.
  • Using EasyMC or public accounts is not allowed.
  • All alts must remain on the same island or team.

Security & Attacks

  • DDoS threats or attempts
  • IP grabbers or malicious links
  • Crashing, lagging, or destabilizing the server

These actions result in permanent removal and reports to Mojang and hosting providers.

Final Notes

NovaQuest is committed to fairness, transparency, and community safety. If unsure — ask before acting.
