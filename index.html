Server Rules & Guidelines

Disclaimers

Using any third-party software to gain an unfair advantage is strictly prohibited and may result in punishment. Repeat offenses can lead to harsher penalties. The Founder reserves the right to permanently remove a player from the network if they show no intent to correct their behavior.

Knowledge of Rules

Claiming ignorance of the rules is not a valid excuse. All rules listed are basic expectations commonly enforced across games and online communities. By playing on NovaQuest, you agree to follow these standards.

Rule Changes

Rules may be updated over time with input from the Management Team but will always be finalized by the Founder. All efforts are made to keep the rules clear, fair, and airtight.

Loopholes & Exploits

Attempting to exploit loopholes or use creative methods to bypass rules is not allowed. Depending on the situation, this may result in punishment or the rule being revised to prevent further abuse.

  • Helpers: Warnings, kicks, mutes
  • JrMods: Temporary & permanent punishments, system tools
  • Mods: Authority over Helpers & JrMods, can issue/reverse punishments
  • SrMods: Advanced punishment controls, IP actions, reviews lower staff
  • Admins+: Unrestricted permissions, confidential & high-risk commands
