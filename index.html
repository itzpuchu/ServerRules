NovaQuest

Official Server Rules

Important Notice

By playing on NovaQuest, you agree to follow all rules listed below. Failure to read the rules is not an excuse.

Rules apply to all players regardless of rank, playtime, or intent.

General Conduct

  • Harassment, threats, or discrimination are not allowed.
  • Toxic behavior, baiting, or provoking staff or players is punishable.
  • Impersonating staff or other players is not allowed.
  • Encouraging others to break rules counts as breaking them yourself.

Unfair Advantage

Any method that gives an unfair advantage over normal gameplay is prohibited.

  • Hacked or modified clients
  • Cheats (fly, speed, x-ray, nuker, reach, etc.)
  • Macros or automation removing player effort
  • Exploits, glitches, or unintended mechanics
  • Mods or tools that provide hidden or unfair information

If something gives an advantage other players do not have, assume it is not allowed.

Competitive Violations

Competitive players are held to stricter standards.

  • 1st offense: 3-day ban + stat wipe + competitive season ban
  • 2nd offense: 7-day ban + stat wipe + competitive season ban
  • 3rd offense: 14-day ban + stat wipe + competitive season ban

Regular players will receive bans without stat wipes, increasing per offense.

Alternate Accounts

  • Alternate accounts are not allowed without approval.
  • Shared IP households must open a ticket.
  • Using alts to bypass punishment results in escalated penalties.

Server & Player Attacks

  • Attempting to lag, crash, or destabilize the server
  • DDoS threats or attempts
  • IP grabbers, malicious links, or doxxing

Punishment includes IP ban, permanent removal, and reports to Mojang and hosting providers.

IRL Trading

Trading anything involving real-world money or external items is not allowed.

Attempting, suggesting, or offering IRL trades results in permanent network removal.

Gambling

  • Player-run casinos or betting systems are prohibited
  • Advertising gambling is not allowed
  • Only official NovaQuest EULA-compliant systems are permitted

Final Notes

NovaQuest is committed to fairness, safety, and transparency. If you are unsure whether something is allowed — ask first.
