Players caught cheating in competitive modes will face **three consequences**:

  • Temporary Ban: 3-day ban from all server activities.
  • Stats Removal: All competitive stats and leaderboard points will be wiped to maintain fairness.
  • Season Competitive Ban: Players cannot participate in competitive matches for the remainder of the current season.

These measures protect the integrity of competitive leaderboards and ensure fairness for all players. Repeat violations may result in longer penalties or permanent disqualification from competitive play.
