NovaQuest Server Rules

These rules exist to keep NovaQuest fair, safe, and enjoyable for everyone.

General Enforcement Policy

  • Ignorance of the rules is not a valid excuse.
  • Joking, sarcasm, or roleplay does not exempt you from punishment.
  • Using loopholes to bypass rules is prohibited.
  • Staff may punish harmful behavior even if not explicitly listed.
  • Punishments may escalate based on intent, impact, or history.

Minor Chat Offenses

Warn → 1h Mute → 12h Mute → 1d Mute

  • Spamming or flooding chat
  • Symbol or character spam
  • Encouraging chat spam
  • Misleading users
  • Minor NSFW references

Major Chat Offenses

3d → 5d → 7d → 14d Mute

  • Toxic or hostile language
  • Hate speech or slurs
  • Explicit NSFW content
  • Leaking personal information
  • Sensitive topics

Severe Chat Offenses

30d Mute → Permanent Mute → Permanent Ban

  • Death threats or encouraging self-harm
  • Racism or discrimination
  • Advertising other servers or services

Unfair Advantage

3d → 7d → 14d → 30d Ban

  • Hacked clients or modified gameplay
  • X-Ray, Fly, Speed, Reach, KillAura
  • Macros, scripts, automation
  • AFK grinding or failing staff checks
  • Boosting other players

Competitive Violations

3-Day Ban + Stat Wipe + Leaderboard Removal + Competitive Ban

  • Any unfair advantage affecting competitive modes

Permanent & IP-Ban Offenses

  • DDoS or DDoS threats
  • Doxxing or threats
  • Botting or mass automation
  • Stalking or grooming behavior

Market & IRL Trading

  • IRL trading (money or services)
  • Attempting or suggesting counts
  • IRL or Buycraft scamming
